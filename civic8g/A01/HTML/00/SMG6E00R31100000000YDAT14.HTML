Side Sill Outer Panel Removal

  • Cut and pry off the side sill outer panel, and replace the center pillar outer separator.
  • If there is any damage to the center pillar, cut the outer panel.

 

  • Check the side sill stiffener and center pillar stiffener positions, and check for damage.
  • If necessary, remove the center pillar lower stiffener, and side sill stiffener, and replace them.
  • When removing the center pillar lower stiffener, replace the center pillar inner separator.
