M/T Shift Lever Housing Replacement

1.Remove the rear center console.

2.Remove the front center console.

3.Remove the harness clips (A).

 

4.Remove the shift lever cover (B).

5.Expand the clamps (A) on the shift lever housing (B), and remove it from groove (C).

 

6.Turn the cables (D) toward arrow make as shown and remove it if from the shift lever housing.

7.Remove the select cable and shift cable as shown.

8.Remove the shift lever housing.

 

9.Install the shift lever housing in the reverse order of removal.

10.Install the front center console.

11.Install the rear center console.
