Taillight Replacement

Taillight


1.Remove the cover (A) and remove the two screws from the taillight (B).

Brake/Taillight: 21/5 W 
Rear Turn Signal Light: 21 W 

 

2.Carefully pull out the taillight, and disconnect the connectors (C) from the taillight.

3.Install the taillight in the reverse order of removal.

Inner Taillight (Lid Light)


1.Remove the tailgate trim panel.

2.Disconnect the connector (A) from the inner taillight (lid light) (B).

Brake/Taillight: 21/5 W 

 

3.Remove the nuts and inner taillight (lid light).

4.Install the inner taillight (lid light) in the reverse order of removal.

Inner Taillight (Rear Panel Light)


1.Remove the tailgate trim panel.

2.Disconnect the connectors (A) from the inner taillight (rear panel light) (B).

Back-up Light: 18 W 
Rear Fog Light: 21 W 

 

3.Remove the nuts and inner taillight (rear panel light).

4.Install the inner taillight (rear panel light) in the reverse order of removal.
