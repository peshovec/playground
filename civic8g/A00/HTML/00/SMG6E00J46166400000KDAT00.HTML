Rear Seat Removal/Installation

NOTE:
  • Have an assistant help you when removing and installing the rear seat.
  • Take care not to scratch the trim and panels or tear the seat covers.
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.

1.Remove the headrests and the center headrest.

2.Release the fasteners (A), then pull back the seat-back cover (B). Pass the rear seat belt buckles (C) through the holes in the seat-back cover.

 

3.Right rear seat: Remove the center seat belt lower anchor bolt.

4.Fold both rear seats down.

5.Release the fasteners, then pull back the edge of the rear floor mat.

6.Left rear seat: Remove the center seat belt buckle tension spring bolt.

7.Left rear seat: Detach the left rear seat belt switch harness clip.

8.Remove the bolts (A) and nuts (B) securing the rear seat.

9.Remove the right rear seat (A) and the left rear seat (B) through both rear door openings.

 

 

10.Install the seat in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:

  • Before installing the anchor bolt, make sure there are no twists or kinks in the center seat belt.
  • Tighten the seat mounting bolts (A) and nuts (B) to the specified torque in the sequence shown.
