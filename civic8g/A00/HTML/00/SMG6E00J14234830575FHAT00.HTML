HFT-Voice Control Switch Test/Replacement

1.Remove the steering wheel.

2.Remove the HFT-voice control switch.

 


3.Measure resistance between the No. 3 and No. 4 terminals in each switch position according to the table.

HFT-Voice Control Switch
Position Resistance 
OFF About 1 kW 
HFT Talk button pressed About 227 kW 
HFT Back button pressed About 47 kW 


4.If the resistance is not as specified, replace the switch.
