Rear Door Latch Replacement

NOTE: Put on gloves to protect your hands.

Latch Replacement


1.Remove these items:

  • Door panel
  • Door outer handle

2.Remove the screws, then remove the panel bracket (A).

 

3.Disconnect the power door look actuator connector (A) and the clips (B).

 

4.Remove the plug caps (C), then remove the plastic cover (D), as needed.

5.Remove the bolt securing the rear lower channel (A).

 

6.Remove the screws (A, B) securing the latch (C), then remove the latch out from between the rear lower channel (D) and the door. Take care not to bend the outer handle rod (E), latch cable (F), and inner handle cable (G).

 

7.Install the latch in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:

  • Make sure the connector is plugged in properly, and each rod is connected securely.
  • Make sure the door locks and opens properly.

Cable Replacement


1.Remove the latch.

2.Remove the screw, and remove the latch protector (A), then disconnect the latch cable (B) and inner handle cable (C).

 

3.Install the cables in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:

  • Make sure the latch cable and inner handle cable are connected securely.
  • Make sure the door locks and opens properly.
