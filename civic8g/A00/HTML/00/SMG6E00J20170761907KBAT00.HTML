Under-cowl Panel Replacement

NOTE: Take care not to scratch the body.

1.Remove these items:

  • Windshield wiper arms
  • Front fender trim, both sides
  • Cowl covers

2.Remove the bolts, release the engine compartment wire harness (A) from a hook (B), then remove the under-cowl panel stay (C) and the under-cowl panel (D).

 

3.Install the under-cowl panel in the reverse order of removal.
