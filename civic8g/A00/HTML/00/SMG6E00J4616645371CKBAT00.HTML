Rear Seat Lock Replacement

NOTE:
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  • Take care not to tear the seams or damage the seat covers.

1.Remove the rear seat.

2.Disassemble the rear seat.

3.Remove the seat-back cove/pad.

4.Remove the bolts securing the rear seat lock (A).

 

5.Remove the wire tie (A). Disconnect the seat lock rod (B) from the rear seat lock (C).

 

6.Release the pivot (A) of the lock knob (B) from the seat-back frame.

 

7.Install the rear seat lock in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:

  • Replace the wire tie you removed with a new one.
  • Make sure the seat lock rod is connected securely.
  • Make use the rear seat locks and unlocks properly.
