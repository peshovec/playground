Front Inner Fender Replacement

NOTE: Take care not to scratch the body.

1.Remove the front strake.

2.Remove the front wheel arch protector.

3.Remove the front inner fender (A).

-1 Remove the clip (B) securing the engine undercover (C), and front inner fender.
-2 From the wheel arch, remove the clips (D) securing the front inner fender (and engine undercover) to the body.
-3 Release the hook (E) of the engine undercover, then remove the front inner fender.

 

4.Install the inner fender in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:

  • Check for damaged or stress-whitened clips, and if necessary, replace them with new ones.
  • Push the clips into place securely.
