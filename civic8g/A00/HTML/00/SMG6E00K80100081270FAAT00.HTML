DTC Troubleshooting: 27

DTC 27:  Steering Angle Sensor


1.Turn the ignition switch OFF.

2.Disconnect the steering angle sensor 5P connector.

3.Disconnect the VSA modulator-control unit 46P connector.

4.Check for continuity between VSA modulator-control unit 46P connector terminal No. 33, No. 35 and steering angle sensor 5P connector terminal No. 5, No. 1 individually.

 

Is there continuity?


YES - Go to 5.


NO - Repair open in the wire between the VSA modulator-control unit and the steering angle sensor.n

5.Turn the ignition switch ON (II).

6.Measure the voltage between body ground and VSA modulator-control unit 46P connector terminal No. 33, No. 35 individually.

 

Is there 0.1 V or more?


YES - Repair short to power in the wire between the VSA modulator-control unit and steering angle sensor.n


NO - Go to 7.

7.Turn the ignition switch OFF.

8.Check for continuity between VSA modulator-control unit 46P connector terminal No. 5, No. 17, No, 32 and steering angle sensor 5P connector terminal No. 4, No. 3, No. 2 individually.

 

Is there continuity?


YES - Go to 9.


NO - Repair open in the wire between the VSA modulator-control unit and the steering angle sensor.n

9.Turn the ignition switch ON (II).

10.Measure the voltage between body ground and VSA modulator-control unit 46P connector terminal No. 5, No, 17, No. 32 individually.

 

Is there 0.1 V or more?


YES - Repair short to power in the wire between the VSA modulator-control unit and the steering angle sensor.n


NO - Go to 11.

11.Turn the ignition switch OFF.

12.Check for continuity between body ground and VSA modulator-control unit 46P connector terminals No. 5, No. 17, No. 32, No. 33, and No. 35 individually.

 

Is there continuity?


YES - Repair short to body ground in the wire between the VSA modulator-control unit and the steering angle sensor.n


NO - Go to 13.


13.Substitute a known-good steering angle sensor.

14.Reconnect all of disconnected connectors.

15.Turn the ignition switch ON (II).

16.Clear the DTC with the HDS.

17.Test-drive the vehicle around a number of corners.

18.Check for DTCs with the HDS.

Is DTC 27 indicated?


YES - Check for loose terminals in the VSA modulator-control unit 46P connector. If necessary, substitute a known-good VSA modulator-control unit and retest.n


NO - Replace the original steering angle sensor.n
