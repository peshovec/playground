Power Steering Assist Check

NOTE: This test should be done with original equipment tyres and wheels at the correct tyre pressure.

1.Start the engine, and let it idle.

2.Attach a commercially available spring scale to the steering wheel. With the engine idling and the vehicle on a clean, dry floor, pull the scale as shown, and read it as soon as the tyres begin to turn.

 

3.If the scale reads no more than 29 N (3.0 kgf, 6.6 lbf), the power assist is OK. If it reads more, check these items:

  • Steering linkage
  • Rack guide adjustment
  • EPS system
