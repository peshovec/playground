Dashboard Vent Removal/Installation

Driver's Vent


NOTE:
  • Take care not to scratch the dashboard and related parts.
  • RHD is shown; LHD is symmetrical.

1.Remove the instrument panel.

2.Remove the screws securing the driver's vent (A), then remove the driver's vent from the instrument panel (B).

 

3.Install the vent in the reverse order of removal.

Center Vent


NOTE: Take care not to scratch the dashboard and related parts.

1.Remove the audio unit.

2.Remove the screws, and release the hooks (A), then remove the center vent (B).

 

3.Install the vent in the reverse order of removal.

Passenger's Vent


NOTE:
  • Take care not to scratch the dashboard and related parts.
  • When removing components, use the trim tools or equivalent.
  • RHD is shown; LHD is symmetrical.

1.Insert the trim tool into a gap between the passenger's vent (A) and the dashboard.

 

2.Gently pull out the passenger's vent (A) to release the hooks (B) and tabs (C), then remove the vent.

 

3.Install the dashboard vent in the reverse order or removal.
