Navigation Unit Removal/Installation

NOTE: If the navigation unit is replaced or disconnected, a map match must be done.

1.Remove the glove box.

2.Remove the nuts and bolt, and wire harness clips then pull out the navigation unit (A).

 

3.Disconnect the connectors (B), then remove the navigation unit.

4.Remove the screws and the bracket (A).

 

5.Install the navigation unit in the reverse order of removal.
