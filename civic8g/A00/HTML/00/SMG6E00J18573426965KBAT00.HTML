Front Fender Trim Replacement

NOTE:
  • Take care not to scratch the fender trim and body.
  • When removing components, use the trim tools or equivalent.

1.Remove the front emblem cover.

2.On right side, disconnect the washer tube (A) and remove the washer tube clip (B). Then remove the clips from the front fender trim (C).

 


3.Remove the front fender trim (A).

 

4.Install the fender trim in the reverse order of removal, and note there items:

  • Replace the clips fastening the fender trim with new ones.
  • Push the clips into place securely.
