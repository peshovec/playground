Front Fender Enclosure Replacement

1.Remove the front inner fender as needed.

2.Open the front door. Remove the clip from inside the door.

 

3.From the wheel arch, release the clips (A, B), then remove the front fender enclosure (C).

 

4.Install the fender enclosure in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:

  • Check for damaged or stress-whitened clips, and if necessary, replace them with new ones.
  • Before installing the clip in the door opening area, install the front fender enclosure (A) to the front fender (B) properly as shown.
  • Push the clips into place securely.
