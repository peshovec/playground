Sunshade Calibration

NOTE: The sunshade calibration must be performed when any of the following events have occurred.
  • The sunshade motor is replaced with a new one.
  • Any component related to the sunshade replaced.
    • Sunshade bracket
    • Sunshade cables, etc.

The sunshade will reverse while it is closing


1.Turn the ignition switch ON (II).

2.Press and hold the sunshade CLOSE switch until the sunshade stops in fully close position.

3.Release the CLOSE switch. Within 5 seconds, press and hold the CLOSE switch again.

NOTE: The sunshade moves to fully open position, then it moves to fully close position.

4.Calibration is complete.

The sunshade will stop while it is operated


1.Turn the ignition switch ON (II).

2.Press and hold the sunshade CLOSE switch.

NOTE: The sunshade will stop while it is operated.

3.Release the CLOSE switch.

4.Press and hold the sunshade CLOSE switch until the sunshade stops in fully close position.

5.Release the CLOSE switch. Within 5 seconds, press and hold the CLOSE switch again.

NOTE: The sunshade moves to fully open position, then it moves to fully close position.

6.Calibration is complete.
