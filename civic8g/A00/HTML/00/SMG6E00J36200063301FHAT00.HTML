Navigation Voice Control Switch Test/Replacement

1.Remove the steering wheel.

2.Remove the voice control switch.

3.Measure the resistance between the No. 2 and No. 4 terminals in each switch position according to the table.

 

Position Resistance 
OFF About 10 kW 
TALK About 2.7 kW 
BACK About 680 W 


4.If the resistance is not as specified, replace the voice control switch.

5.Check for continuity between the terminals in each switch position according to the table.

 

6.If the continuity check is not as specified, replace the switch.
