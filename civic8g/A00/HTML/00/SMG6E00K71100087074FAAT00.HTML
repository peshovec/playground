DTC Troubleshooting: G+AUTO

DTC indicator G and AUTO:  Driver's Air Mix Control Motor Failure


1.Clear the DTC.

2.Turn the ignition switch OFF, and then ON(II).

3.Do the self-diagnostic with the climate control unit.

4.Check for DTCs.

Is DTC G and AUTO indicated?


YES - Replace the driver's air mix control motor.n


NO - Intermittent failure.n
