Engine Undercover Replacement

NOTE: Take care not to scratch the body.

1.Remove the engine undercover (A).

-1 Remove the clips (B) that secure the front inner fender (C) and engine undercover to the body.
-2 From under the front bumper (D), remove the bolts (E) and the clips (F).
-3 Remove the bolts (G) that secure the floor undercover (H) and engine undercover to the body.
-4 From under the body, remove the bolts (I) and the clips (J).
-5 Release the hooks (K) of the engine undercover, then pull the engine undercover out.

 

2.Install the engine undercover in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:

  • Check for damaged or stress-whitened clips, and if necessary, replace them with new ones.
  • Push the clips into place securely.
