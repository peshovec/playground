Dashboard Center Upper Lid Removal/Installation

NOTE:
  • Take care not to scratch the dashboard and related parts.
  • Do not pry the panel surface with any tools to prevent damage.
  • When removing components, use the trim tools or equivalent.
  • RHD is shown; LHD is symmetrical.

1.Pry up on the outside edge of the dashboard center upper lid (A) at the notch (B) with a trim tool.

 

2.Gently pull out the bottom of the dashboard center upper lid (A) to detach the clips, then remove the lid.

Without navigation system 

With navigation system 

3.Install the dashboard center upper lid in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:

  • Check for damaged or stress-whitened clips, and if necessary, replace them with new ones.
  • Push the clips into place securely.
