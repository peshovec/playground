Rear Seat Armrest Replacement

For Some Models


NOTE: Take care not to tear the seams or damage the seat covers.

1.Gently pull out the pivot cover (A) to release the hook (B), then remove the pivot cover.

 

2.Using a 17 mm socket wrench, remove the pivot bolts (A), then remove the wave washer (B), washer (C), and nylon washer (D). Remove the stopper screw (E).

 

3.Remove the pivot shaft (A) from the bushing (B) on the right, then remove the armrest (C).

 

4.If necessary, remove the bushing (A).

 

5.Install the armrest in the reverse order of removal. Replace the bushing if it is damaged or stress-whitened.
