DTC Troubleshooting: 97

DTC 97:  VSA System


1.Turn the ignition switch ON (II).

2.Check for DTCs with the HDS.

Is another DTC indicated?


YES - Do the appropriate troubleshooting for the DTC.n


NO - Go to 3.


3.Clear the DTC with the HDS.

4.Test-drive the vehicle in a straight line. Accelerate the vehicle up to 10 km/h (6 mph), and stop, then accelerate up to 10 km/h (6 mph).

Is DTC 97 indicated?


YES - Replace the VSA modulator-control unit.n


NO - Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Check for loose terminals at the VSA modulator-control unit 46P connector. Refer to intermittent failures troubleshooting.n
