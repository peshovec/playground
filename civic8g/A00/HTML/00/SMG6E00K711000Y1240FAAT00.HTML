DTC Troubleshooting: B1240 (L)

DTC B1240 or DTC indicator L:  A Problem in the Mode Control Linkage, Doors, or Motor


1.Clear the DTC with the HDS.

2.Turn the ignition switch OFF, and then ON (II).

3.Do the self-diagnostic with the HDS or climate control unit.

4.Check for DTCs.

Is DTC D and AUTO?


YES - Go to troubleshooting DTC D and AUTO.


NO - Go to 5.


5.Check for DTCs.

Is DTC B1240 or L indicated?


YES - Go to 6.


NO - Intermittent failure.n


6.Turn the ignition switch OFF.

7.Remove the mode control motor.

8.Check the mode control motor linkage and door for smooth movement.

Is the linkage and door OK?


YES - Replace the mode control motor.n


NO - Repair the mode control linkage and door.n
