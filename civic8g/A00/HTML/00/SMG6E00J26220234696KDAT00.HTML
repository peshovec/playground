Instrument Lower Panel Removal/Installation

NOTE:
  • Take care not to scratch the dashboard and related parts.
  • Do not pry the panel surface with any tools to prevent damage.
  • When removing components, use the trim tools or equivalent.
  • RHD is shown; LHD is symmetrical.

1.Tilt the steering column down.

2.Gently pull out the bottom of the instrument lower panel (A) to detach the clips, and disconnect the in-car temperature sensor connector (B) and air hose (C).

 

3.Install the instrument lower panel in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:

  • Check for damaged or stress-whitened clips, and if necessary, replace them with new ones.
  • Make sure the in-car temperature sensor connector is plugged in properly, and the air hose id connected securely.
  • Push the clips into place securely.
