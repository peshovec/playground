Yaw Rate-Lateral Acceleration Sensor Replacement

NOTE:
  • Do not damage or drop the sensor as it is sensitive.
  • Do not use air or electric impact tools.

1.Remove the rear center console.

2.Disconnect the yaw rate-lateral acceleration sensor 4P connector (A).

 

3.Remove the sensor mounting nuts, and remove the yaw rate-lateral acceleration sensor (B).

4.Install in the reverse order of removal.
