DTC Troubleshooting: B1238 (K)

DTC B1238 or DTC indicator K:  A Problem in the Passenger's Air Mix Control Linkage, Door, or Motor


1.Clear the DTC with the HDS.

2.Turn the ignition switch OFF, and then ON (II).

3.Do the self-diagnostic with the HDS or climate control unit.

4.Check for DTCs.

Is DTC M and AUTO indicated?


YES - Go to troubleshooting DTC M and AUTO.


NO - Go to 5.


5.Check for DTCs.

Is DTC B1238 or K indicated?


YES - Go to 6.


NO - Intermittent failure.n


6.Turn the ignition switch OFF.

7.Remove the passenger's air mix control motor.

8.Check the passenger's air mix control linkage and door for smooth movement.

Is the linkage and door OK?


YES - Replace the passenger's air mix control motor.n


NO - Repair the passenger's air mix control linkage and door.n
