DTC Troubleshooting: 11-01

DTC 11-01:  Low/High IG1-terminal Voltage (Initial diagnosis)


1.Clear the DTCs with the HDS.

2.Turn the ignition switch OFF.

3.Start the engine.

4.Wait at least 60 seconds.

Does the EPS indicator come on?


YES - Go to 5.


NO - Check for loose wires or poor connections. If the connections are good, the system is OK at this time.n


5.Turn the ignition switch OFF.

6.Disconnect the EPS control unit connector D (28P).

7.Turn the ignition switch ON (II).

8.Wait at least 60 seconds, measure the voltage between EPS control unit conenctor D (28P) terminal No. 16 and body ground.

 

If the voltage between 9.63-16.6 V?


YES - Replace the EPS control unit.


NO - Check for a poor connection at the battery terminal or battery. If the connections are good, go to the alternator regulator circuit troubleshooting: L13A7 engine model, R18A2 engine model.n
