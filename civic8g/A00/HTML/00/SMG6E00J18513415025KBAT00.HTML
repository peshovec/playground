Rear Bumper Reflector Replacement

NOTE:
  • Take care not to scratch the rear bumper and body.
  • Put on gloves to prevent damage.

1.Remove the rear bumper.

2.Push out the clip (A), release the hook (B), then remove the rear bumper reflector (C).

 

3.Reattach the hook to the rear bumper first, and push the clip into place securely.
