Rear Bumper Finisher Disassembly/Reassembly

NOTE:
  • Take care not to scratch the finisher and related parts.
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.

1.Remove the rear bumper.

2.Remove the bolts, then remove the inner finisher (A) and the exhaust finisher (B) from the outer finisher (C).

 

3.Reassemble the finisher in the reverse order of disassembly.
