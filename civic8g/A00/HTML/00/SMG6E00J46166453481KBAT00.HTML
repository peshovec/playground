Rear Seat Control Cable Replacement

NOTE:
  • Put on gloves protect your hands.
  • Take care not to rear the seams or damage the seat covers.

1.Remove the rear seat.

2.Disassemble the rear seat.

3.Remove the seat cushion cover/pad.

4.Remove the wire tie (A). Disconnect the control cable (B), then remove the cable. Take care not to bend the cable.

 

5.Install the cable in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:

  • Replace the wire tie you removed with a new one.
  • Make sure the control cable is routed properly.
  • Make sure the control cable is connected securely.
