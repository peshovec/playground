Headlight Adjuster Motor Replacement

Removal


1.Remove the headlight assembly, then remove the headlight bulb.

2.Paste the tape (A) behind of the caps (B).

 

3.Remove the caps by striking with the hammer.

4.Remove the two torx screws from the headlight adjuster motor (A).

 

5.Wrap the clip band (B) around the headlight adjuster motor.

6.Pull the clip band (A), then remove the headlight adjuster motor (B).

 

7.Disconnect the 3P connector (C) from the headlight adjuster motor.

Installation


1.Reconnect the 3P connector (A), and install the headlight adjuster motor (B).

 

2.Tighten the two torx screws, then install the new caps (C).

3.Pull the reflector (A), then install the pivot (B) to the fixing clip (C).

 

4.Confirm the following:

  • The reflector does not shake.
  • The connector is attached to a prescribed clamping.
  • There is no garbage in the housing.

5.Install the headlight bulb and headlight assembly.

6.Do the headlight learning procedure.
