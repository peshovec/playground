Rear Seat Foot Pipe Replacement

NOTE: Put on gloves to protect your hands.

1.Remove the rear seat.

2.Disassemble the rear seat.

3.Remove the seat cushion cover/pad.

4.Disconnect the control cable (A) from the foot pipe (B).

 

5.On both sides, release the push nuts (A), then remove the set pins (B).

 

6.Remove the foot pipe (A) from the seat cushion frame (B).

 

7.Install the foot pipe in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:

  • Make sure the control cable is connected securely.
  • Replace the push nuts you removed with new ones.
