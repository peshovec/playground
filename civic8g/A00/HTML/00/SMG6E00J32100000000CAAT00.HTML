Exterior Lights System Description

Headlight Adjuster System (with HID)


Automatic headlights adjuster system is composed of the headlight adjuster control unit, front and rear headlight adjuster leveling sensors, and left and right headlight leveling motors.
The system detects the vehicle posture every three minutes during running constantly with the headlight switch ON, and after eight seconds when the vehicle is stopped running with the ignition switch ON (II) and headlight switch ON.
If the vehicle posture has been changed, adjust the headlight vertical position automatically.

 


Headlight Adjuster Leveling Sensors


The front headlight adjuster leveling sensor is located between the front suspension sub-frame and the stabilizer bar connecting by shaft, and rear headlight adjuster leveling sensor is located between right rear suspension sub-frame and the right side rear damper connecting by the shaft. Detects the vehicle posture and send a voltage signals (about 0.4 V to 4.6 V) to the headlight adjuster control unit.

Headlight Adjuster Control Unit


The headlight adjuster control unit is located in the driver's dashboard lower cover. The headlight adjuster control unit send a voltage signal that is based on the voltage signal (about 2 V to 11 V) from the sensor to the left and right headlight leveling motors.

Headlight Adjuster Motors


The headlight leveling motor is located behind the left and right headlights and adjusters the headlights vertical position by receiving the voltage signal (about 2 V to 11 V) from the headlights adjuster control unit.

Headlight Adjuster System (without HID)


Manual headlight adjuster system composed of the headlight adjuster switch and headlight adjuster motors.
In this system, headlights can be adjusted to the number of passenger's and the load by operating the switch.

Headlight Adjuster Switch


The headlight adjuster switch is located at the right of dashboard. The headlights can be adjusted by operating the switch according to the posture change by the load that hangs to the vehicle.

Headlight Adjuster Motors


The headlight adjuster motors are used the same one as the automatic headlights adjuster system.
