Glove Box Damper Replacement

NOTE:
  • Take care not to scratch the dashboard and related parts.
  • RHD is shown; LHD is symmetrical.

1.Remove the glove box.

2.Release the glove box damper wire (A).

 

3.Remove the screws, then remove the glove box damper (A).

 

4.Install the damper in the reverse order of removal.
