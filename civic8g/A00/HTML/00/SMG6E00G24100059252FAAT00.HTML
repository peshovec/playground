Temperature Control Switch Circuit (Passenger's) Troubleshooting

1.Turn the ignition switch OFF.

2.Test the passenger's temperature control switch.

Is the passenger's temperature control switch OK?


YES - Go to 3.


NO - Replace the passenger's temperature control switch.n


3.Disconnect the climate control unit 32P connector.

4.Check for continuity between the following terminals of the climate control unit 32P connector and the passenger's temperature control switch 5P connector.

32P: 5P: 
No. 1 No. 5 
No. 2 No. 4 
No. 3 No. 3 

 

Is there continuity?


YES - Go to 5.


NO - Repair any open in the wires between the climate control unit and the passenger's temperature control switch.n

5.Check for continuity between body ground and the climate control unit 32P connector terminals No. 1, 2, and No. 3 individually.

 

Is there continuity?


YES - Repair any short to body ground in the wires between the climate control unit and the passenger's temperature control switch.n


NO - Go to 6.


6.Check for continuity between the climate control unit 32P connector terminals as follows.

From terminal To terminals 
1 2, 3 
2 3 


Is there continuity between any of the terminals?


YES - Repair the short in the wires.n


NO - Go to 7.

7.Turn the ignition switch ON (II), and check the same terminals for voltage to body ground.

 

Is there any voltage?


YES - Repair any short to power in the wires between the climate control unit and the passenger's temperature control switch. This short may also damage the climate control unit. Repair the short to power before replacing the  climate control unit.n


NO - Check for loose wires or poor connections at the climate control unit 32P connector and at the passenger's temperature control switch 5P connector. If the connections are good, substitute a known-good climate control unit, and recheck. If the symptom/indication goes away, replace the original  climate control unit.n
