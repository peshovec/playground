DTC Troubleshooting: B1168

DTC B1168:  Gauge Control Module Lost Communication with the ECM (ENG Message)


1.Clear the DTCs with the HDS.

2.Turn the ignition switch OFF, and then back ON (II).

3.Wait for 6 seconds or more.

4.Check for DTCs with the HDS.

Is DTC B1168 indicated?


YES - Go to 5.


NO - Intermittent failure. The gauge control module (tach) is OK at this time.n


5.Check for DTCs with the HDS.

Are all of DTCs B1168, B1170, B1178, B1183 and B1187 also indicated at the same time?


YES - Troubleshoot DTC B1178.n


NO - Go to 6.

6.Check for PGM-FI system DTCs with the HDS.

Is any DTCs indicated?


YES - Go to the indicated troubleshooting.n


NO - Go to 7.


7.Turn the ignition switch OFF.

8.Disconnect the gauge control module (tach) 36P connector.

9.Disconnect the ECM connector A (44P).
  • R18A2 engine
  • L13A7 engine

10.Connect the gauge control module (tach) 36P connector No. 1 and No. 19 terminals and body ground with jumper wires.

 

11.Check for continuity between the ECM connector A (44P) terminals No. 36 and No. 37 and body ground individually.

 

Are there continuity?


YES - Update the ECM if it does not have the latest software, or substitute a known-good ECM (R18A2 engine, L13A7 engine), and recheck. If DTC B1168 is indicated again, replace the gauge control module (tach).n


NO - Repair an open in the wire.n
