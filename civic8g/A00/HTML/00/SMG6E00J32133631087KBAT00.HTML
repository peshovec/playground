Headlight Adjuster Leveling Sensor Replacement

With HID


1.Raise the vehicle and support it with safety stands in proper location.

2.Disconnect the 3P connector (A) from the front or rear headlights adjuster leveling sensor (B).

Front 

Rear 

3.Remove the mounting bolt(s) and nut from the headlights adjuster leveling sensor.

4.Install in the reverse order of removal, and go to headlight learning procedure (With HID).
