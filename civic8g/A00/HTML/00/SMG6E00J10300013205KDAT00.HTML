Audio-HVAC Subdisplay Unit Removal/Installation

With Navigation


1.Remove the navigation display.

2.Remove the screws and the audio-HVAC subdisplay unit (A).

 

3.Install in the reverse order of removal.

Without Navigation


1.Remove the instrument upper lid, and the gauge control module (speedo).

2.Remove the screws, then pull out the audio-HVAC subdisplay unit (A).

 

3.Disconnect the connector (B).

4.Install in the reverse order of removal.
