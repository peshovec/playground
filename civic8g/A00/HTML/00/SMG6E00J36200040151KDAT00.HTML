Navigation Microphone Removal/Installation

NOTE:
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  • Take care not to scratch the dashboard and related parts.
  • Lay a workshop towel under the parts when working on them to protect the face panel from scratches or other damage.

1.Remove the frontindividual map light and the lenses.

2.Remove the cover (A).

 

3.Remove the microphone (A).

 

4.Install the microphone in the reverse order of removal.
