Instrument Panel Removal/Installation

NOTE:
  • Take care not to scratch the dashboard and related parts.
  • Do not pry the panel surface with any tools to prevent damage.
  • When removing components, use the trim tools or equivalent.
  • RHD is shown; LHD is symmetrical.

1.Remove the instrument lower panel.

2.Remove the screws (A), and detach the clips (B) along the edge of the instrument panel (C).

 

3.Gently pull out the instrument panel (A) to disconnect the climate control connector (B), VSA OFF switch connector (C), head light adjuster switch connector (D) and the engine start switch connector (E).

 

4.Install the instrument panel in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:

  • Replace any damaged clips.
  • Make sure all connectors are plugged in properly.
  • Push the clips into place securely.
