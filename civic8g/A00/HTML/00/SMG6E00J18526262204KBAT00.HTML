Floor Undercover Replacement

NOTE: Take care not to scratch the rear bumper and body.

1.Remove the floor undercover (A).

-1 Remove the bolts (B) that secure the engine undercover (C) and floor undercover to the body.
-2 Remove the bolts (D), and detach the clips (E), then remove the floor undercover.

NOTE: To remove the clip E, pry the inner pin up at the edge near the line (F) on its head.

 

2.Install the undercover in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:

  • Check for damaged or stress-whitened clips, and if necessary, replace them with new ones.
  • Push the clips into place securely.
