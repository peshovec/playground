Door Lock Knob Switch Test

Driver's Door


1.Remove the driver's door panel.

2.Disconnect the 10P connector (A) from the actuator (B).

 

3.Check for continuity between the terminals.

  • There should be continuity between the No. 9 [No. 6] and No. 5 [No. 10] terminals when the door lock knob switch is in the LOCK position and no continuity when the switch is in the UNLOCK position.
  • There should be continuity between the No. 8 [No. 7] and No. 5 [No. 10] terminals when the door lock knob switch is in the UNLOCK position and no continuity when the switch is in the LOCK position.

[ ]: RHD type 


4.If the continuity is not as specified, replace the door lock actuator.

Passenger's/Rear Door


1.Remove the passenger's door panel or rear door panel.

2.Disconnect the 10P connector (A) from the actuator (B).

 

3.Check for continuity between the terminals.

  • There should be continuity between the No. 8 [No. 7] and No. 10 [No. 5] terminals when the door lock knob switch in the UNLOCK position.
  • There should be no continuity when the door lock knob switch is in the LOCK position.

[ ]: Front passenger's door (RHD type) and left rear door 


4.If the continuity is not specified, replace the door lock actuator.
