DTC Troubleshooting: 53

DTC 53:  Motor Stuck ON


1.Turn the ignition switch ON (II).

Is the pump motor working?


YES - Replace the ABS modulator-control unit.n


NO - Go to 2.


2.Measure the voltage between ABS modulator-control unit 26P connector terminal No. 26 and body ground.

 

Is there less than 0.1 V?


YES - Go to 3.


NO - Repair open in the wire between the ABS modulator-control unit and body ground (G202).n

3.Clear the DTC with the HDS.

4.Test-drive the vehicle at 15 km/h (10 mph) or more.

5.Check for DTCs with the HDS.

Is DTC 53 indicated?


YES - Check for loose terminals in the ABS modulator-control unit 26P connector. If necessary, substitute a known-good ABS modulator-control unit and retest.n


NO - Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Check for loose terminals at the ABS modulator-control unit 26P connector. Refer to intermittent failures troubleshooting.n
