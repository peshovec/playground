DTC Troubleshooting: 61

DTC 61:  IG1 Voltage Low


1.Test the battery and check the connections.

Are the battery and connections OK?


YES - Go to 2.


NO - Check the battery performance, and do the alternator and regulator circuit troubleshooting: L13A7 engine model, R18A2 engine model.n


2.Turn the ignition switch OFF.

3.Disconnect the VSA modulator-control unit 46P connector.

4.Turn the ignition switch ON (II).

5.Measure the voltage between VSA modulator-control unit 46P connector terminal No. 40 and body ground.

 

Is there battery voltage?


YES - Go to 6.


NO - Repair open (or high resistance) in the wire between No. 4 (7.5 A) fuse in the under-dash fuse/relay box and the VSA modulator-control unit.n


6.Turn the ignition switch OFF.

7.Reconnect the VSA modulator-control unit 46P connector.

8.Turn the ignition switch ON (II).

9.Clear the DTC with the HDS.

10.Test-drive the vehicle at 15 km/h (10 mph) or more.

11.Check for DTCs with the HDS.

Is DTC 61 indicated?


YES - Check for loose terminals in the VSA modulator-control unit 46P connector. If necessary, substitute a known-good VSA modulator-control unit and retest.n


NO - Intermittent failure, the system is OK at this time. Check for loose terminals at the VSA modulator-control unit 46P connector. Refer to intermittent failures troubleshooting.n
