Fuel Fill Door Replacement

NOTE: Take care not to scratch the body.

1.Remove the there items:

  • Trunk side trim panel, left side
  • Fuel fill door latch

2.Using a T20 Torx bit, remove the cap (A) by turning it counterclockwise, and remove the screw.

 

3.Pull back the fuel fill door (A) to release the hooks (B) and grommet (C), then remove it from the body.

 

4.Install the fuel fill door in the reverse order of removal.
