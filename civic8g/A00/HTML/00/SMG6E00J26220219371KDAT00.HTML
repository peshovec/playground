Dashboard Passenger's Undercover Removal/Installation

NOTE:
  • Take care not to scratch the dashboard and related parts.
  • RHD is shown; LHD is symmetrical.

1.Remove the passenger's dashboard undercover (A).

-1 Gently pull down the cover to detach the clips.
-2 Pull the cover away to release the hook (B).

 

2.Install the cover in the reverse order of removal, and note these items:

  • Check for damaged or stress-whitened clips, and if necessary, replace them with new ones.
  • Push the clips into place securely.
