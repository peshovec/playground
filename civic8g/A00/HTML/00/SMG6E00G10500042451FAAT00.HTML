Climate Control/Navigation Communication Line Circuit Troubleshooting

1.Operate the climate control system in several modes.

Is the climate control system OK?


YES - Go to 2.


NO - Do the self-diagnostic with the HDS or climate control unit.n


2.Do the Navi system link.

Is the Air-Con icon red?


YES - Go to 3.


NO - Go to 9.


3.Turn the ignition switch OFF.

4.Disconnect navigation unit connector A (20P).

5.Disconnect the climate control unit 32P connector.

6.Check for continuity between the following terminals of the climate control unit 32P connector and navigation unit connector A (20P).

32P: 20P: 
No. 5 No. 4 
No. 6 No. 14 
No. 21 No. 15 

 

Is there continuity?


YES - Go to 7.


NO - Repair any open in the wires between the climate control unit and the navigation unit.n

7.Check for continuity between body ground and the climate control unit 32P connector terminals No. 5, 6, and No. 21 individually.

 

Is there continuity?


YES - Repair any short to body ground in the wires between the climate control unit and the navigation unit.n


NO - Go to 8.


8.Reconnect the climate control unit 32P connector.

9.Disconnect navigation unit connector A (20P).

10.Turn the ignition switch ON (II).

11.Connect the climate control unit 32P connector terminals No. 5, 6 and No. 21 with a jumper wire.

 

12.Press the RECIRCULATION CONTROL and OFF buttons.

Does the RECIRCULATION CONTROL indicator turn on?


YES - Do the Unit check with the navigation system.n


NO - Substitute a known-good climate control unit, and recheck. If the symptom goes away, replace the original  climate control unit.n


13.Disconnect the jumper wire.
