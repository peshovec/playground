Climate Control Symptom Troubleshooting

Symptom Diagnostic procedure Also check for 
Blower, heater controls, and A/C do not work Climate control power and ground circuit troubleshooting 
  • HVAC DTCs
  • Blown fuse No. 36 (10 A) in the under-dash fuse/ relay box
  • Poor ground at G503
  • Cleanliness and tightness of all terminals
 
Voice communication does not work Navigation communication line circuit troubleshooting 
  • HVAC DTCs
  • Cleanliness and tightness of all connectors
 
Both fans do not run at low speed with the A/C on (but the A/C compressor runs with the A/C on) Radiator and A/C condenser fan low speed circuit troubleshooting 
  • HVAC DTCs
  • Blown fuse No. 18 (20 A) in the under-hood fuse/relay box, and No. 36 (10 A) in the under-dash fuse/relay box
  • Poor ground at G301
  • Cleanliness and tightness of all terminals
 
The A/C condenser fan does not run at high speed (but both fans run at low speed and the A/C compressor operates with the A/C on) A/C condenser fan high speed circuit troubleshooting 
  • HVAC DTCs
  • Blown fuse No. 15 (20 A) and No. 20 (7.5 A) in the under-hood fuse/relay box
  • Cleanliness and tightness of all terminals
 
Both fans do not run at high speed with the A/C on (but both fans run at low speed and the A/C compressor operates with the A/C on) A/C pressure sensor troubleshooting (L13A7 engine): A/C pressure sensor circuit low voltage, A/C pressure sensor circuit high voltage
A/C pressure sensor troubleshooting (R18A2 engine): A/C pressure sensor circuit low voltage, A/C pressure sensor circuit high voltage
ECT troubleshooting (L13A7 engine): ECT sensor 2 circuit low voltage, ECT sensor 2 circuit high voltage
ECT troubleshooting (R18A2 engine): ECT sensor circuit low voltage, ECT sensor circuit high voltage 
  • Powertrain DTCs, L13A7 engine, R18A2 engine
  • Cleanliness and tightness of all terminals
 
The A/C compressor clutch does not engage (but both fans run with the A/C on) A/C compressor clutch circuit troubleshooting 
  • HVAC DTCs
  • Blown fuse No. 7 (7.5 A) in the under-hood fuse/relay box, and No. 36 (10 A) in the under- dash fuse/relay box
  • Cleanliness and tightness of all terminals
 
A/C system does not come on (both fans and the A/C compressor do not work); heater is OK A/C signal circuit troubleshooting
A/C pressure sensor troubleshooting (L13A7 engine): A/C pressure sensor circuit low voltage, A/C pressure sensor circuit high voltage
A/C pressure sensor troubleshooting (R18A2 engine): A/C pressure sensor circuit low voltage, A/C pressure sensor circuit high voltage 
  • HVAC DTCs.
  • Powertrain DTCs, L13A7 engine, R18A2 engine
  • Cleanliness and tightness of all terminals
 
Blower fan runs slower than expected in cold weather ECT troubleshooting (L13A7 engine): ECT sensor 2 circuit low voltage, ECT sensor 2 circuit high voltage
ECT troubleshooting (R18A2 engine): ECT sensor circuit low voltage, ECT sensor circuit high voltage
NOTE: It is normal for the blower to run slowly until the engine coolant temperature begins to rise. If the blower continues to run slowly for an abnormal length of time, continue to troubleshoot the problem. 		Powertrain DTCs, L13A7 engine, R18A2 engine 
Passenger's temperature control switch does not work Passenger's temperature control switch circuit troubleshooting Cleanliness and tightness of all terminals 
HDS does not communicate with the climate control unit or the vehicle Troubleshoot the DLC circuit, L13A7 engine, R18A2 engine
