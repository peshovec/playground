Clutch Damper Replacement

NOTE:
  • Use fender covers to avoid damaging painted surfaces.
  • Do not spill brake fluid on the vehicle; it may damage the paint; if brake fluid does contact the paint, wash it off immediately with water.
  • SPLM manual transmission model is shown, SPHM maunal transmission model is similar.

1.Write down the frequencies for the radio's preset buttons. Disconnect the negative (-) cable first, then the positive (+) cable from the battery. Remove the battery.

2.Remove the air cleaner housing.

3.Disconnect the clutch hose (A) and clutch pipe (B).

 


4.Remove the clutch damper bracket mounting bolts.

 

5.Remove the clutch damper (A) from the clutch damper bracket (B).

 

6.Install the clutch damper (A) to the clutch damper bracket (B).

 

7.Install the clutch damper bracket mounting bolts.

 

8.Connect the clutch pipe (A) and clutch hose (B). Use the new sealing washers (C).

 

9.Bleed the clutch hydraulic system.

10.Check the clutch operation and check for leaking fluid.

11.Install the battery.

12.Enter the anti-theft code for the radio, then enter the customer¢s radio station presets.

13.Perform the power window control unit reset procedure.
