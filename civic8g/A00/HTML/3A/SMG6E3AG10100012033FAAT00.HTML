A/C Condenser Fan High Speed Circuit Troubleshooting

NOTE:

  • Do not use this troubleshooting procedure if the A/C compressor is inoperative. Refer to the symptom troubleshooting index.
  • Before doing any symptom troubleshooting, check for powertrain DTCs, L13A7 engine, R18A2 engine.

1.Check the No. 15 (20 A) and No. 20 (7.5 A) fuses in the under-hood fuse/relay box.

Are the fuses OK?


YES - Go to 2.


NO - Replace the fuses, and recheck. If fuses blow again, check for a short in the No. 15 (20 A) and No. 20 (7.5 A) fuses circuit.n


2.Jump the SCS line with the HDS.

3.Disconnect ECM connector A (44P).

4.Turn the ignition switch ON (II).

5.Measure the voltage between ECM condenser A (44P) terminal No. 5 and body ground.

 

Is there battery voltage?


YES - Go to 6.


NO - Repair open in the wire between the under-hood fuse/relay box and the ECM. If the wire is OK, replace the under-hood fuse/relay box.n

6.Turn the ignition switch OFF.

7.Connect the ECM connector A (44P) terminal No. 5 to body ground with a jumper wire.

 

8.Turn the ignition switch ON (II).

Does the A/C condenser fan run on high?


YES - Check for loose wires or poor connections at ECM connector A (44P) terminal No. 5. If the connections are good, substitute a known-good ECM, and recheck. If the symptom/indication goes away, replace the original ECM, L13A7 engine, R18A2 engine.n


NO - Replace the under-hood fuse/relay box.n
