PTC Heater Core Replacement



Do not apply current when core is removed
  • To prevent electrocution because current will flow through the whole PTC heater core.
  • If any foreign material is stuck, a stench, smoke or fire could occur.



1.Remove the dashboard.

2.Disconnect the connector (A) then remove the connector holder (B). Remove the self-tapping screws and PTC heater core (C) from the heater unit.

 

3.Install the core in the reverse order of removal. Do not pull the PTC heater core harness from a connection to prevent a circuit malfunction or harness burning.
