DTC Troubleshooting: P1027 (172-8), P1028 (178-8), P1035 (183-8), P1060 (179-8), P1383 (173-8), P1405 (182-8)

DTC P1027:  Turbocharger Boost Control Solenoid Circuit Overheat
  
  
DTC P1035:  Fuel Heater Relay Circuit Overheat
  
  
DTC P1383:  Glow Plug Control Module Circuit Overheat
  
  


NOTE: Before you troubleshoot, record all freeze data and review the general troubleshooting information.

1.Check the heat source around ECM.

Is there heat source around ECM?


YES - Remove the heat source, then go to 2.


NO - Go to 2.


2.Make a temperature in the cabin sufficiently low.

3.Turn the ignition switch ON (II).

4.Reset the ECM with the HDS.

5.Check for Temporary DTCs or DTCs with the HDS.

Are DTC P1027, P1028, P1035, P1060, P1383 and/or P1405 indicated?


YES - Update the ECM if it does not have the latest software, or substitute a known-good ECM, then recheck. If the symptom/indication goes away with a known-good ECM, replace the original ECM.n


NO - Intermittent failure, system is OK at this time.n
