Engine Oil Filter Housing Replacement

1.Remove the engine cover.

2.Remove the drive belt.

3.Remove the alternator.

4.Remove the A/C compressor. Do not disconnect the A/C line from the compressor.

5.Remove the thermostat housing.

6.Remove the oil filter housing assembly (A).

 

7.Remove the oil filter assembly, engine oil cooler, drive belt auto-tensioner, and water pump, then replace the oil filter housing.

8.Reassemble the oil filter housing reverse order of removal, then install the oil filter housing using the new O-rings (B).

9.Install the thermostat housing.

10.Install the A/C compressor.

11.Install the alternator.

12.Install the drive belt.

13.Install the engine cover.
