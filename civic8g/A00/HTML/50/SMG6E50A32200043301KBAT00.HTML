Engine Oil Filter Replacement

1.Remove the oil filter assembly using the oil filter wrench (A) (P/N 04151-RBD-305 or equivalent). Use only socket type oil filter wrench to avoid damage to the oil filter cap.

 

2.Separate the oil filter cap and oil filter element.

3.Install the new O-rings (A) and oil filter element (B) to the oil filter cap (C).

 

4.Apply new engine oil around the O-rings, then install the oil filter assembly to the oil filter housing.

5.Tighten the oil filter assembly using the oil filter wrench (P/N 04151-RBD-305 or equivalent).

Specified Torque: 
25 N·m (2.5 kgf·m, 18 lbf·ft)
