Radiator Fan High Speed Circuit Troubleshooting

1.Check the No. 15 (30 A) fuse in the under-hood fuse/relay box.

Is the fuse OK?


YES - Go to 2.


NO - Replace the fuse, and recheck.n


2.Disconnect under-hood fuse/relay box connector No. 3 (20P) and radiator fan motor 2P connector.

3.Check for continuity between the under-hood fuse/relay box connector No. 3 terminal No. 11 and the radiator fan motor 2P connector terminal No. 2.

 

Is there continuity?


YES - Go to 4.


NO - Repair an open in the wire between the under-hood fuse/relay box connector No. 3 terminal No. 11 and the radiator fan motor 2P connector terminal No. 2.n

4.Check for continuity between the radiator fan motor 2P connector terminal No. 1 and body ground.

 

Is there continuity?


YES - Go to 5.


NO - Check for an open in the wire between the radiator fan motor 2P connecter terminal No. 1 and body ground. If the wire is OK, check for poor ground at G301.n


5.Test the radiator fan motor.

Is the radiator fan motor OK?


YES - Replace the under-hood fuse/relay box.n


NO - Replace the radiator fan motor.n
