CMP Pulse Plate Replacement

1.Remove the camshaft position (CMP) sensor.

2.Remove the cylinder head cover.

3.Hold the intake camshaft with an open-end wrench, then loosen the CMP pulse plate mounting bolt.

 

4.Remove the CMP pulse plate.

 

5.Install the CMP pulse plate and bolt.

6.Hold the intake camshaft with an open-end wrench, then tighten the bolt.

Specified Torque: 
6 x 1.0 mm 
12 N·m (1.2 kgf·m, 8.7 lbf·ft) 


7.Install the cylinder head cover.

8.Install the CMP sensor.
