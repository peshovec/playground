A/C Condenser Fan High Speed Circuit Troubleshooting

NOTE:
  • Do not use this troubleshooting procedure if the radiator fan and/or the A/C compressor is inoperative. Refer to the symptom troubleshooting index.
  • Before doing any symptom troubleshooting, check for powertrain DTCs.

1.Remove the fan control relay from the under-hood fuse/relay box, and test it.

Is the relay OK?


YES - Go to 2.


NO - Replace the fan control relay.n


2.Turn the ignition switch ON (II).

3.Measure the voltage between the fan control relay 5P socket terminal No. 5 and body ground.

 

Is there battery voltage?


YES - Go to 4.


NO - Replace the under-hood fuse/relay box.n


4.Turn the ignition switch OFF.

5.Check for continuity between the fan control relay 5P socket terminal No. 2 and body ground.

 

Is there continuity?


YES - Go to 6.


NO - Check for an open in the wire between the fan control relay and body ground. If the wire is OK, check for poor ground at G301.n

6.Jump the SCS line with the HDS.

7.Disconnect ECM connector No. 2 (58P).

8.Check for continuity between the fan control relay 5P socket terminal No. 3 and ECM connector N0. 2 (58 P) terminal No. 54.

 

Is there continuity?


YES - Check for loose wires or poor connections at ECM connector No. 2 (58 P). If the connections are good, substitute a known-good ECM, and recheck. If the symptom/indication goes away, replace the original ECM.n


NO - Repair open in the wire between the fan control relay and the ECM.n
