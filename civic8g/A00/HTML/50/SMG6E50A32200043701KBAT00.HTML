Engine Oil Replacement

1.Warm up the engine.

2.Remove the maintenance lid.

 

3.Remove the drain bolt (A), and drain the engine oil.

 

4.Reinstall the drain bolt with a new washer (B).

5.Refill the engine with the recommended oil.

Capacity 
At Oil Change: 
5.5 L (5.8 US qt, 4.8 Imp qt) 
At Oil Change including Filter: 
5.9 L (6.2 US qt, 5.2 Imp qt) 
After Engine Overhaul: 
6.8 L (7.2 US qt, 6.0 Imp qt) 

 

6.Run the engine for more than 3 minutes, then check for oil leakage.
