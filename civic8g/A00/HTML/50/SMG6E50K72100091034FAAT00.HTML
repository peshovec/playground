DTC Troubleshooting: P1034 (183-7)

DTC P1034:  Fuel Heater Relay Circuit Shorted to Power


NOTE: Before you troubleshoot, record all freeze data and review the general troubleshooting information.

1.Turn the ignition switch ON (II).

2.Clear the DTC with the HDS.

3.Do the FUEL HEATER ON/OFF in the INSPECTION MENU with the HDS.

4.Check for Temporary DTCs or DTCs with the HDS.

Is DTC P1034 indicated?


YES - Go to 5.


NO - Intermittent failure, system is OK at this time. Check for poor connections or loose terminals at the under-hood fuse/relay box and the ECM.n


5.Turn the ignition switch OFF.

6.Remove the under-hood fuse/relay box.

7.Check the fuel heater relay.

Is the fuel heater relay OK?


YES - Go to 8.


NO - Replace the under-hood fuse/relay box, then go to 12.


8.Disconnect ECM connector No. 2 (58P).

9.Reconnect all connectors except the 10P connector to the under-hood fuse/relay box.

10.Turn the ignition switch ON (II).

11.Measure voltage between ECM connector No. 2 terminal No. 41 and body ground.

 

Is there battery voltage?


YES - Repair short to power in the wire between the under-hood fuse/relay box and ECM connector No. 2 terminal No. 41, then go to 11.


NO - Substitute a known-good under-hood fuse/relay box, then go to 12 and recheck. If DTC P1034 is not indicated, replace the original under-hood fuse/relay box, then go to 12. If DTC P1034 is indicated, go to 17.


12.Reconnect all connectors.

13.Turn the ignition switch ON (II).

14.Reset the ECM with the HDS.

15.Do the FUEL HEATER ON/OFF in the INSPECTION MENU with the HDS.

16.Check for Temporary DTCs or DTCs with the HDS.

Are any Temporary DTCs or DTCs indicated?


YES - If DTC P1034 is indicated, check for poor connections or loose terminals at the under-hood fuse/relay box and the ECM, then go to 1. If any other Temporary DTCs or DTCs are indicated, go to the indicated DTC's troubleshooting.


NO - Troubleshooting is complete.n

17.Update the ECM if it does not have the latest software, or substitute a known-good ECM.

18.Do the FUEL HEATER ON/OFF in the INSPECTION MENU with the HDS.

19.Check for Temporary DTCs or DTCs with the HDS.

Are any Temporary DTCs or DTCs indicated?


YES - If DTC P1034 is indicated, check for poor connections or loose terminals at the under-hood fuse/relay box and the ECM, then go to 1. If any other Temporary DTCs or DTCs are indicated, go to the indicated DTC's troubleshooting.


NO - If the ECM was updated, troubleshooting is complete. If the ECM was substituted, replace the original ECM.n
