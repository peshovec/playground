PTC Heater Circuit Troubleshooting

NOTE: Before doing any symptom troubleshooting, check for powertrain DTCs.

1.Check the No. 26, 27, 28 (40 A) fuses in the under-hood fuse/relay box, and the No. 36 (10 A) fuse in the under-dash fuse/relay box.

Are the fuses OK?


YES - Go to 2.


NO - Replace the fuses and recheck. If the fuses blow again, check for a short in the No. 26, 27, 28 (40 A) and No. 36 (10 A) fuses circuit.n


2.Disconnect the PTC heater core 7P connector.

3.Turn the ignition switch ON (II).

4.Measure the voltage between the PTC heater core 7P connector terminal No. 1 and body ground.

 

Is there battery voltage?


YES - Go to 5.


NO - Repair open in the wire between the under-hood fuse/relay box and the PTC heater core. If the wire is OK, replace the under-hood fuse/relay box.n


5.Turn the ignition switch OFF.

6.Check for continuity between body ground and the PTC heater core 7P connector terminals No. 4, 5, 6, and 7 individually.

 

Is there continuity?


YES - Go to 7.


NO - Check for open in the wires between the PTC heater core and body ground at G401.n


7.Turn the ignition switch ON (II).

8.Measure the voltage between the climate control unit 32P connector terminal No. 17 and body ground.

 

Is there battery voltage?


YES - Go to 9.


NO - Repair open in the wire between the under-hood fuse /relay box and the climate control unit. If the wire is OK, replace the under-hood fuse /relay box.n

9.Measure the voltage between the PTC heater core 7P connector terminal No. 3 and body ground.

 

Is there battery voltage?


YES - Go to 10.


NO - Repair open in the wire between the under-hood fuse/relay box and the PTC heater core. If the wire is OK, replace the under-hood fuse/relay box.n


10.Measure the voltage between the climate control unit 32P connector terminal No. 18 and body ground.

 

Is there battery voltage?


YES - Go to 11.


NO - Repair open in the wire between the under-hood fuse/relay box and the climate control unit. If the wire is OK, replace the under-hood fuse /relay box.n

11.Measure the voltage between the PTC heater core 7P connector terminal No. 2 and body ground.

 

Is there battery voltage?


YES - Go to 12.


NO - Repair open in the wire between the under-hood fuse/relay box and the PTC heater core. If the wire is OK, replace the under-hood fuse /relay box.n


12.Measure the voltage between the climate control unit 32P connector terminal No. 19 and body ground.

 

Is there battery voltage?


YES - Go to 13.


NO - Repair open in the wire between the under-hood fuse/relay box and the climate control unit. If the wire is OK, replace the under-hood fuse/relay box.n


13.Substitute a known-good climate control unit, and recheck.

Is the symptom goes away?


YES - Replace the original  climate control unit.n


NO - Replace the PTC heater core.n
