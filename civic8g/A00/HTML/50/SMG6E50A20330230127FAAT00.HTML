Glow Plug Indicator Circuit Troubleshooting

1.Turn the ignition switch ON (II).

2.Do the gauge self-diagnostic function.

Does the glow plug indicator flash?


YES - Update the ECM if it does not have the latest software, then recheck.n


NO - Substitute a known-good gauge control module (tachometer), and recheck. If the MIL circuit is OK, replace the original gauge control module (tachometer).
