Vacuum Pump Replacement

1.Remove the intermediate shaft.

2.Remove the oil bypass pipe.

3.Remove the chain case and cam chain.

4.Disconnect the vacuum hose.

5.Remove the bolts (A).

 

6.Remove the vacuum pump (B).

7.Install the sensor in the reverse order of removal with a new O-ring (C).

NOTE: When remove the vacuum pump, make sure the O-ring (D) is in place.
