Intercooler Replacement

Exploded View


 

1.Remove the engine undercover.

2.Remove the front bumper.

3.Remove the intercooler intake hose (A) and intercooler outlet hose (B).

 

4.Remove the duct.

 

5.Remove the intercooler lower bracket (A), and remove the intercooler (B).

 


6.Remove the intercooler outlet pipe (A).

 

7.Install the intercooler outlet pipe with new gasket (B).

8.Install the intercooler in the reverse order of removal. Make sure the upper and lower cushions are set securely.

9.Install front bumper.

10.Install the engine undercover.
