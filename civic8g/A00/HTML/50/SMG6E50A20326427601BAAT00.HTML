Fuel Filter Water Bleeding

1.Remove the air cleaner.

2.Place a suitable container under the fuel filter to catch up the water.

3.Connect the hose (A) to water screw (B).

 

4.Loosen the water screw, and bleed the water from the fuel filter.

5.Tighten the water screw securely, and disconnect the hose.

6.Start the engine, and then check showing water in fuel filter (A) in the information display (B).

  • If the water in fuel filter display is on, go to 7.
  • If the water in fuel filter display is not on, fuel filter water bleeding is complete.

 

7.Disconnect the fuel filter water level switch 2P connector, and then recheck the water in fuel filter display.

  • If the water in fuel filter display is on, repair short in the wire between the fuel filter water level switch and the gauge control module (tach).
  • If the water in fuel filter display is not on, replace the fuel filter water level switch.

8.Bleed the fuel system.
