DTC Troubleshooting: P0088 (95-11)

DTC P0088:  Fuel Rail Pressure Too High


NOTE: Before you troubleshoot, record all freeze data and review the general troubleshooting information.

1.Turn the ignition switch ON (II).

2.Clear the DTC with the HDS.

3.Start the engine. Hold the engine speed at 2,000rpm (min-1) with no load (in neutral) until the radiator fan comes on, then let it idle.

4.Rise the engine speed slowly applies for 30 seconds.

5.Check the CKP NOISE COUNT in the DATA LIST with the HDS.

Is 1 count or more indicated?


YES - Check for damage on the CKP sensor pulser plate. If needed, replace the CKP sensor pulser plate, then go to 8.


NO - Go to 6.


6.Do the FUEL RAIL PRESSURE TEST in the INSPECTION MENU with the HDS.

Is the result OK?


YES - Go to 7.


NO - Replace the fuel supply pump assembly, then go to 8.

7.Do the FUEL PRESSURE CONTROL VALVE TEST in the INSPECTION MENU with the HDS.

Is the result OK?


YES - Intermittent failure, system is OK at this time.n


NO - Replace the fuel supply pump assembly, then go to 8.


8.Turn the ignition switch ON (II).

9.Reset the ECM with the HDS.

10.Start the engine. Hold the engine speed at 2,000rpm (min-1) with no load (in neutral) until the radiator fan comes on, then let it idle.

11.Check for Temporary DTCs or DTCs with the HDS.

Are any Temporary DTCs or DTCs indicated?


YES - If DTC P0088 is indicated, check for poor connections or loose terminals at the FRP sensor and the ECM, then go to step 1. If any other Temporary DTCs or DTCs are indicated, go to the indicated DTC's troubleshooting.


NO - Troubleshooting is complete.n
