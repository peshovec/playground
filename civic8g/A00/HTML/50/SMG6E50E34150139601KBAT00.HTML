M/T Clutch Housing Mainshaft Oil Seal Replacement

Special Tools Required
Attachment, 42 x 47 mm (  07746-0010300)
 

Driver (  07749-0010000)
 



1.Remove the mainshaft assembly.

2.Remove the oil seal from the clutch housing. Be careful when removing the seal to avoid damaging the clutch housing.

 

3.Drive in the new oil seal from the transmission side using the special tools.
