Engine Oil Pan Installation

1.Remove any old liquid gasket from the oil pan mating surfaces, bolts and bolt holes.

2.Clean, and dry the oil pan mating surfaces.

3.Install the new oil pan gasket on the oil pan.

 

4.Apply liquid gasket, P/N 08C70-K0334M or 08C70- X0331S, to the engine block mating surface of the oil pan and the inner threads of the holes.

NOTE:
  • Apply a 1.5 mm wide bead of the liquid gasket along the broken line (A).
  • Apply a 5.0 mm wide bead of the liquid gasket to the shaded area (B).
  • Do not install components if 5 minutes or more have passed after applying the liquid gasket. Instead, remove the old residue and reapply the liquid gasket.

 

5.Install the oil pan (A) with dowel pin (B) and new
O-ring (C).

 

6.Tighten the bolts in two or three steps.

Specified torque
(1): 24 N·m (2.4 kgf·m, 17 lbf·ft) 
(2)-(13): 12 N·m (1.2 kgf·m, 8.7 lbf·ft) 


NOTE:
  • Wait at least 30 minutes before filling the engine with oil.
  • Do not run the engine for at least 3 hours after installing the oil pan.

 

7.Wipe off the excess liquid gasket on the each side of crankshaft pulley and flywheel.

8.Install the clutch cover (A), and tighten the two bolts securing the transmission.

 

9.Install the dipstick tube (A) with new O-ring (B), then install the dipstick.

 

10.Connect the oil level sensor connector (A), then install the oil level sensor covers (B).

 

11.Install the bolt (C) securing the harness holder.

12.Install the oil pan cover (A) and engine under guard (B).

 

13.If the engine is still in the vehicle, do the following steps.

14.Install the lower torque rod.

15.Install the engine undercover.

16.Install the A/C compressor, then connect the A/C compressor clutch connector (With A/C).

17.Install the drive belt, and adjust it (With A/C).

18.Refill the engine with engine oil.
