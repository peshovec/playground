Cylinder Head Rocker Arm and Shaft Disassembly/Reassembly

NOTE:

  • Identify each parts as it is removed so that each item can be reinstalled in its original position.
  • When disassembling, or reassembling the rocker arms, remove or install the rocker arm from cam chain side on the rocker shaft.
  • Inspect the rocker arm shaft and rocker arms.
  • If reused the rocker arms must be installed in the same positions.
  • Prior to reassembling, clean all the parts in solvent, dry them, and apply lubricant to any contact points.
