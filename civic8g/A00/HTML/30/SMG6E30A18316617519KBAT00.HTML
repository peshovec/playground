CKP Pulse Plate Replacement

NOTE: Keep the cam chain away from magnetic fields.

1.Remove the front wheels.

2.Remove the engine undercover.

3.Remove the drive belt.

4.Remove the cylinder head cover.

5.Set the No. 1 piston at top dead center (TDC). The ‘‘UP'' mark on the camshaft sprocket should be at the top, and the TDC grooves on the camshaft sprocket should line up with the top edge of the head.

6.Remove the alternator bracket mounting bolt, and loosen the alternator mounting bolt.

7.Remove the idler pulley.

8.Remove the water pump pulley.

9.Remove the crankshaft pulley.

10.Remove the oil pan.

11.Disconnect the crankshaft position (CKP) sensor connector, then remove the harness clamps.

12.Support the engine with a jack and wood block under the engine block.

13.Remove the ground cable, then remove the side engine mount bracket.

14.Keep alternator away of the chain case. Remove the chain case.

15.Remove the pulse plate (A).

 

16.Install the CKP pulse plate.

17.Check the chain case oil seal for damage. If the oil seal is damaged, replace the chain case oil seal.

18.Remove old liquid gasket from the chain case mating surfaces, bolts, and bolt holes.

19.Clean, and dry the chain case mating surfaces.

20.Apply liquid gasket, P/N 08C70-K0234M, 08C70-
K0334M or 08C70-X0331S, to the engine block mating surface of the chain case.

NOTE:
  • Apply a 1.5 mm wide bead of the liquid gasket along the broken line.
  • Apply a 3.0 mm wide bead of the liquid gasket to the engine block upper surface contact areas (B) on the chain case.
  • Do not install the components if 5 minutes or more have passed after applying the liquid gasket. Instead, remove the old residue and reapply the liquid gasket.

21.Install the chain case.

NOTE:
  • Wait at least 30 minutes before filling the engine with oil.
  • Do not run the engine for at least 3 hours after installing the chain case.

22.Install the harness clamps, and connect the CKP sensor connector.

23.Install the side engine mount bracket, then loosely tighten the new nuts, and loosely tighten the bolt. Install the ground cable.

24.Remove the air cleaner housing assembly.

25.Loosen the transmission mounting bolt and nuts.

26.Tighten the side engine mount mounting bolt and nuts.

27.Tighten the transmission mounting bolt and nuts.

28.Install the oil pan.

29.Install the crankshaft pulley.

30.Install the cylinder head cover.

31.Install the water pump pulley.

32.Install the idler pulley.

33.Install the alternator bracket mounting bolt.

34.Install the drive belt and adjust it.

35.Do the crankshaft position (CKP) pattern clear/CKP pattern learn procedure.
