Camshaft Installation

1.Install the camshaft (A) into the cylinder head, then install the camshaft thrust cover (B) with new O-ring (C).

 

2.Install the camshaft position (CMP) sensor with new O-ring, then connect the CMP sensor connector.

3.Install the harness holder, then install harness holder mounting bolt, ground cable and air cleaner housing bracket.

4.Install the camshaft sprocket.

5.Install the rocker arm assembly.

6.Install the air cleaner housing.
