Starter Removal and Installation

Removal


1.Disconnect the negative cable from the battery, then disconnect the positive cable.

2.Remove the engine undercover.

3.Disconnect the starter cable (A) from the B terminal, then disconnect the connector (B) from the S terminal.

 

4.Remove the two bolts securing the starter, then remove the starter.

Installation


1.Install the starter to the engine. Install the starter cable (A) and connector (B). Make sure the starter cable crimped side of the ring terminal is facing out.

 

2.Install the engine undercover.

3.Connect the positive cable to the battery first, then the negative cable.

4.Start the engine to make sure the starter works properly.

5.Set the clock.

6.Do the power window control unit reset procedure.
