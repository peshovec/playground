Transmission Mount Replacement

1.Remove the air cleaner housing assembly.

2.Remove the engine control module (ECM) cover, then remove the three bolts securing the ECM.

3.Remove the ECM bracket.

4.Remove the under hood fuse/relay box from the bracket.

5.Support the engine with a 300 x 300 x 25 mm (12 x 12 x 1 in.) piece of wood and a jack under the oil pan.

NOTE: Do not attach a jack on the center of the oil pan to avoid damage to the oil pan.

6.Remove the transmission mount stiffener (A), then remove the transmission mount (B).

 

7.Install the transmission mount with the new mounting bolts (C), then install the transmission mount stiffener.

8.Loosely tighten the new bolt and nuts (D).

9.Raise the hoist to full height.

10.Remove the engine undercover.

11.Loosen the lower torque rod mounting bolt (A).

 

12.Lower the vehicle on the hoist.

13.Tighten the transmission mounting bolt and nuts.

 

14.Raise the hoist to full height.

15.Tighten the lower torque rod mounting bolt.

 

16.Install the engine undercover.

17.Lower the vehicle on the hoist.

18.Install the under hood fuse/relay box to the bracket.

19.Install the ECM bracket.

20.Install the ECM, then install the ECM cover.

21.Install the air cleaner housing assembly.
