M/T Shift Cable Replacement

1.Remove the air cleaner housing.

2.Disconnect the back-up light switch connector then remove the engine harness cover and the harness clips.

3.Remove the shift cable clips (A) and the cotter pins (B).

 

4.Remove the shift cables (C) from the top of transmission housing.

5.Remove the rear center console.

6.Remove the front center console.

7.Remove the harness clips (A).

 

8.Remove the shift lever cover (B).

9.Expand the clamps (A) on the shift lever housing (B), and remove it from groove (C).

 

10.Turn the cables (D) toward arrow mark as shown and remove it from the shift lever housing.

11.Remove the select cable and shift cable as shown.

 

12.Install the shift cable in the reverse order of removal.
