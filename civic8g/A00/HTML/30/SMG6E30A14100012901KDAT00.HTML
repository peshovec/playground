Alternator Removal and Installation

Removal


1.Disconnect the negative cable from the battery first, then disconnect the positive cable.

2.Remove the drive belt.

3.Remove the air intake duct.

 

4.Disconnect the alternator connector (A) and BLK wire (B) from the alternator.

 

5.Remove the harness clamps (C) from the alternator.

6.Remove the alternator.

 

Installation


1.Install the alternator.

 

2.Connect the alternator connector (A) and BLK wire (B) to the alternator.

 

3.Install the harness clamps (C) to the alternator.

4.Install the air intake duct.

 

5.Install the drive belt.

6.Adjust the belt tension.

7.Connect the positive cable to the battery first, then the negative cable.

8.Set the clock.

9.Do the power window control unit reset procedure.
