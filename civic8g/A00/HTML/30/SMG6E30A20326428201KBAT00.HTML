Fuel Pressure Regulator Replacement

1.Remove the fuel tank unit.

2.Remove the clips (A), and remove the fuel pressure regulator (B).

 

3.Install the part in the reverse order or removal with a new O-rings (C). When installing the fuel tank unit, align the marks on the unit and the fuel tank.

NOTE: Coat the O-ring with clean engine oil. Do not pinch the O-rings during installation. Never apply brake fluid, vegetable oil, or alcohol-based oil to the O-rings.
