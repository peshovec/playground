CMP Pulse Plate Replacement

Removal


1.Remove the air cleaner housing.

2.Remove the air cleaner housing bracket, ground cable and harness holder mounting bolt, then remove the harness holder from the bracket.

3.Remove the cylinder head cover.

4.Remove the camshaft thrust cover.

 

5.Hold the camshaft with an open-end wrench, then loosen the bolt.

 

6.Remove the camshaft position (CMP) pulse plate.

 

Installation


1.Install the CMP pulse plate.

 

2.Hold the camshaft with an open-end wrench, then tighten the bolt to 34 N·m (3.5 kgf·m, 25 lbf·ft).

 

3.Install the camshaft thrust cover (A), use new O-ring (B).

 

4.Install the cylinder head cover.

5.Install the harness holder, then install harness holder mounting bolt, ground cable and air cleaner housing bracket.

6.Install the air cleaner housing.
