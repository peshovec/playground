DTC Troubleshooting: P0351 (71), P0352 (71), P0353 (72), P0354 (72), P0355 (73), P0356 (73), P0357 (74), P0358 (74)

DTC P0351:  No. 1 Cylinder Intake Side Ignition Coil Circuit Malfunction
  
  
DTC P0353:  No. 2 Cylinder Intake Side Ignition Coil Circuit Malfunction
  
  
DTC P0355:  No. 3 Cylinder Intake Side Ignition Coil Circuit Malfunction
  
  
DTC P0357:  No. 4 Cylinder Intake Side Ignition Coil Circuit Malfunction
  
  


NOTE:
  • Before you troubleshoot, record all freeze data and any on-board snapshot, and review the general troubleshooting information.
  • Information marked with an asterisk (*) applies to the rear side ignition coil.

1.Turn the ignition switch ON (II).

2.Clear the DTC with the HDS.

3.Start the engine.

4.Check for Temporary DTCs or DTCs with the HDS.

Is DTC P0351, P0352*, P0353, P0354*, P0355, P0356*, P0357, and/or P0358* indicated?


YES - Go to 5.


NO - Intermittent failure, system is OK at this time. Check for poor connections or loose terminals at the ignition coil and the ECM.n

5.Turn the ignition switch OFF.

6.Exchange the ignition coil from the problem cylinder with one from another cylinder.

7.Start the engine.

8.Check for Temporary DTCs or DTCs with the HDS.

Is DTC indicated at the exchanged cylinder position?


YES - Replace the faulty ignition coil, then go to 25.


NO - Go to 9.


9.Turn the ignition switch OFF.

10.Check the No. 11 IGNITION COIL 2 (15 A) fuse* and the No. 10 IGNITION COIL 1 (15 A) fuse in the under-hood fuse/relay box.

Is the fuse OK?


YES - Go to 11.


NO - Go to 12.


11.Test the ignition coil relay in the under-hood fuse/relay box.

Is the ignition coil relay OK?


YES - Go to 15.


NO - Replace the under-hood fuse/relay box, then go to 25.


12.Disconnect the under-hood fuse/relay box 20P (white) connector.

13.Disconnect all ignition coil 3P connectors.

14.Check for continuity between No. 1 cylinder intake side ignition coil 3P connector terminal No. 3 and body ground.

 

Is there continuity?


YES - Repair short in the wire between the ignition coils and the under-hood fuse/relay box. Also replace the No. 11 IGNITION COIL 2 (15 A) fuse* and the No. 10 IGNITION COIL 1 (15 A) fuse, then go to 25.


NO - Replace the under-hood fuse/relay box, then go to 25.


15.Reconnect the under-hood fuse/relay box 20P (white) connector.

16.Disconnect the ignition coil 3P connector from the problem cylinder.

17.Turn the ignition switch ON (II).

18.Measure voltage between ignition coil 3P connector terminal No. 3 and body ground of the problem cylinder (see table).

YES - Go to 21.


NO - Repair open in the wire between the ignition coil and G101, then go to 25.


21.Jump the SCS line with the HDS.

22.Disconnect ECM connector C (44P).

23.Check for continuity between body ground and the appropriate ECM connector terminal of the problem cylinder (see table).

YES - Repair short in the wire between the ECM and the ignition coil, then go to 25.


NO - Go to 24.

24.Check for continuity between appropriate ignition coil 3P connector terminal No. 1 and the appropriate ECM connector terminal of the problem cylinder (see table).

Is there continuity?


YES - Go to 31.


NO - Repair open in the wire between the ECM and the ignition coil, then go to 25.


25.Turn the ignition switch OFF.

26.Reconnect all connectors and the battery.

27.Turn the ignition switch ON (II).

28.Reset the ECM with the HDS.

29.Do the ECM idle learn procedure.

30.Check for Temporary DTCs or DTCs with the HDS.

Are any Temporary DTCs or DTCs indicated?


YES - If DTC P0351, P0352*, P0353, P0354*, P0355, P0356*, P0357, and P0358* is indicated, check for poor connections or loose terminals at the ignition coil and the ECM, then go to 1. If any other Temporary DTCs or DTCs are indicated, go to the indicated DTC's troubleshooting.


NO - Troubleshooting is complete.n

31.Update the ECM if it does not have the latest software, or substitute a known-good ECM.

32.Check for Temporary DTCs or DTCs with the HDS.

Are any Temporary DTCs or DTCs indicated?


YES - If DTC P0351, P0352*, P0353, P0354*, P0355, P0356*, P0357, and P0358* is indicated, check for poor connections or loose terminals at the ignition coil and the ECM, then go to 1. If any other Temporary DTCs or DTCs are indicated, go to the indicated DTC's troubleshooting.


NO - If the ECM was updated, troubleshooting is complete. If the ECM was substituted, replace the original ECM.n
