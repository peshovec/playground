Cylinder Head Valve Clearance Adjustment

NOTE: Adjust the valves only when the cylinder head temperature is less than 38 °C (100 °F).

1.Inspect the idle speed.

2.Connect the Honda Diagnostic System (HDS) to the data link connector (DLC). Turn the ignition switch ON (II).

3.Make sure the HDS communicates with the vehicle and the engine control module (ECM). If it doesn't, troubleshoot the DLC circuit.

4.Select Health Check, then VALVE CLEARANCE CHECK on the HDS. If necessary, adjust the valve clearance after ample time for the engine to cool off.

5.Remove the cylinder head cover.

6.Set the No. 1 piston at top dead center (TDC). The ‘‘UP'' mark (A) on the camshaft sprocket should be at the top, and the TDC grooves (B) on the camshaft sprocket should line up with the top edge of the head.

 

7.Select the correct thickness feeler gauge for the valves you're going to check.

Valve Clearance 
Intake: 0.17±0.02 mm (0.007±0.008 in.) 
Exhaust: 0.28±0.02 mm (0.011±0.008 in.) 

 

8.Insert the feeler gauge (A) between the adjusting screw and the end of the valve stem on No. 1 cylinder and slide it back and forth; you should feel a slight amount of drag.

 

9.If you feel too much or too little drag, loosen the locknut (A), and turn the adjusting screw (B) until the drag on the feeler gauge is correct.

 

10.Tighten the locknut and recheck the clearance. Repeat the adjustment if necessary.

11.Rotate the crankshaft clockwise. Align the No. 3 piston TDC groove (A) on the camshaft sprocket with the top edge of the head.

 

12.Check, and if necessary, adjust the valve clearance on No. 3 cylinder.

13.Rotate the crankshaft clockwise. Align the No. 4 piston TDC groove (A) on the camshaft sprocket with the top edge of the head.

 

14.Check, and if necessary, adjust the valve clearance on No. 4 cylinder.

15.Rotate the crankshaft clockwise. Align the No. 2 piston TDC groove (A) on the camshaft sprocket with the top edge of the head.

 

16.Check, and if necessary, adjust the valve clearance on No. 2 cylinder.

17.Install the cylinder head cover.
