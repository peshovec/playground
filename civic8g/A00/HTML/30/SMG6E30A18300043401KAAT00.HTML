Engine Oil Pan Removal

1.If the engine is already out of the vehicle, Go to 10.

2.Remove the drive belt (With A/C).

3.Disconnect the A/C compressor clutch connector, then remove the A/C compressor without disconnecting the A/C hoses (With A/C).

4.Raise the vehicle on the hoist to full height.

5.Remove the engine undercover.

6.Drain the engine oil.

7.Support the engine with a 300 x 300 x 25 mm (12 x 12 x 1 in.) piece of wood and a jack under the oil pan.

NOTE: Do not attach a jack on the center of the oil pan to avoid damage to the oil pan.

8.Remove the lower torque rod.

9.Remove the jack.

10.Remove the oil level sensor covers (A), then disconnect the oil level sensor connector (B).

 

11.Remove the bolt (C) securing the harness holder.

12.Remove the dipstick, then remove the dipstick tube.

 

13.Remove the clutch cover (A), and remove the two bolts securing the transmission.

 

14.Remove the engine under guard (A) and oil pan cover (B).

 

15.Remove the bolts securing the oil pan.

16.Using a flat blade screwdriver, separate the oil pan from the block in the places shown.

 

17.Remove the oil pan.
